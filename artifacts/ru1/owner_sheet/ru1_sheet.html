The decisions are 162 changes, 240 kept, and 3 held back (two are a different letter and one is uncertain). The lead confirmed all 30 sampled changes and all 10 sampled keeps. Each card shows three letters: the book's hard sign with its flag (left), the changed letter (middle), and the book's soft sign without a flag (right). There is nothing to review unless a middle letter looks like the right one instead of the left one.
