Line Skew and Ink Check

Second iteration of the reference-free glyph tools on real parliament crops, after the independent review (CP-G1) and your notes. Each line shows the fitted underline, where the letter body leaves the crop, a box per detected letter and, when present, a second text line; below it, how much of the letter body is inside the crop and one ink state per letter: broken or faint below zero, merged with its neighbours above zero.

No OCR reading and no PDF text layer is used anywhere on this page. Everything comes from the pixels of each crop, plus the median letter height of the same document for crops too clipped to measure their own.

SkewAngle of a straight fit through the letters' baseline evidence; positive means the line rises to the right. On 100 rendered straight lines rotated by a known ±2°: median error 0.01°; for the 95 fits marked high confidence the worst is 0.52°. The two large misses (3.8° and 10.4°) were short lines between full-width neighbours, marked medium/low confidence.
BendsThe underline now follows a bend at either end, tracking letter to letter from the straight core. On 100 rendered lines with a known end bend, lines with any column more than a quarter letter-height off fell from 84% to 45%; straight lines are unaffected. Sharp curls in the last one to three letters are still missed.
Ink stateYour rule: a letter is one piece in the middle region, and neighbours do not share columns there. Pieces sharing columns, or fitting one letter width across a small gap, are one broken letter. Rendered lines with injected defects: broken letters caught 82%, calls right 78%. Merged blobs: calls right 99.5%, caught 74%. Faint letters, once invisible to the ink mask, are now detected and flagged.
Letter vs accentCorrected after the review: rates now count every true accent, including lost ones. 97.95% of separate accents are found, at 96.1% precision. In heavily inked renders a quarter of accents touch their letter and become part of it; this does not change the underline or the ink state, which use the middle region.
Two lines in one cropA second band fitted to the ink the main line does not explain. Real fixtures, labelled by me: 7 of 7 double and 0 of 20 single (threshold set on those same lines). Rendered crops: 95% caught, but only 74% of flags right, since rendered neighbour tips are more prominent than real ones. Partial second lines covering a small part of the width are not yet flagged.
Known limitsFits can join two separate pieces of text at different heights (see the −7.2° case). Short lines between full-width neighbours give low-confidence fits. A uniformly faint line is compared with itself, so it reads as intact.
