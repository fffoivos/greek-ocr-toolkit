## Projection and lead choice

`project(label, frozen_codec)` in build_c2.py is a pure function: NFD; remove all Unicode Mn; `str.lower()`; retain Unicode category L* letters present in codec v2.1 plus ASCII space; collapse ASCII spaces and strip. Missing post-lower letters stop the build before filtering, with their characters reported; no codec extension. Nonletters other than ASCII space are discarded, including tabs and newlines (not turned into spaces). The lead choice is **lower, not casefold**: final `ς` remains `ς`, while Python's context-sensitive lowercasing applies to uppercase sigma. `ß`, `æ`, `œ` are retained.

`base_label` is the projected text; `base_label_ids` is a list of uint32 codec atom IDs, with no BOS/EOS/blank insertion.
