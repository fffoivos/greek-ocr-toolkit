## Rule and edit policy

`greekocr/numerics/ink_rule.py` consumes the existing renderer side record, generated by `greekocr.glyphs.components.line_glyphs` and the exact flanking digit top/baseline estimates. Every decision retains component count, full component/union boxes, normalized height/centre/width, token median digit width, gap width, helper x-height band, class, margin, reason and rule hash. Small crossing components remain visible; full-height digit components cannot become dot candidates. A component at least three native pixels wide and at least twice as wide as high is a hard horizontal-stroke veto, including crossing components. Multiple eligible components abstain. An empty nonpositive gap abstains because overlapping boxes cannot prove absent ink.

The fixed screens were specified in code before fitting. Raised boxes search four paddings around fit positives and four margin thresholds, prioritizing nonempty zero-false-positive fit predictions, then precision and recall. ## Scorer and training applier (fixtures only)

`scripts/numerics/n1_gate.py` replays the frozen decisions, validates sealed preparation/public packs, canonical scans, VALID control gates and original-answer custody, then accepts two independent high-confidence agreements or explicit lead rulings. It does not require rule agreement to admit dash truth. Disagreements, holds and unlocalized targets without lead rulings cannot pass. Passing requires ≥99% raised precision with nonzero raised predictions/truth, zero edits on all true hyphen/en/em/any-dash ranges, and complete adjudication. Recall and both selected/all-enumerated unsure rates are reported; sampled read-set recall is explicitly not full-population recall.

Future explicit scoring command (not run):

```sh
python scripts/numerics/n1_gate.py --lead artifacts/n1/n1e/lead_e.jsonl
```

`scripts/numerics/n1_apply_train.py` recomputes the real locked gate rather than trusting a saved passed flag, also requires qualified held-out dev precision, then enumerates every numeric token/boundary of every collection-v2 training row. Crop-bound DocAI digit symbols or a `--geometry` sidecar supply exact localization; absent localization produces per-slot unsure records and no edit. A geometry sidecar is JSONL with `line_id`, `crop_sha256`, `parent_label_sha256` (SHA256 of UTF-8 label), and line-complete `slots` carrying exact `slot_id/start/end/glyph/geometry/digit_widths`; the applier recomputes pixel measurements rather than accepting supplied classes or measurements. Default v2 rows do not contain DocAI symbol geometry, so useful edits require that localized sidecar. Missing or mismatched crops fail closed. No uniform text-to-pixel guess or new reader is introduced.

After the gates pass, the applier writes the complete parent-ID stream `n1_v1_train.jsonl` with parent/new labels, edits, tags, decision-record references and rule hash; the complete `n1_v1_train_decisions.jsonl`; a SHA-bound manifest; and an atomic LQB registry update preserving existing entries. None of those production outputs or the `n1_v1_train` registry entry has been created in this task. The unchanged input is `~/Projects/greek-ocr-toolkit/docs/harvest/collection/v2/import_real_v2_train.jsonl`.
