Accent Fixing Options

Decision memo for the owner · 16 Oct 2026 · spell-checker track (branch opus/spellcheck, head 9cb598dd). Every figure is measured on the 1,264 three-way Wikisource lines against print truth, the blind two-reader pixel adjudication of all 189 Google-vs-Wikisource mark differences. Examples per class: options sheet; adjudication: print-truth sheet.

Recommendation: the combined design, with the money spent on the mark reader. Deterministic pre-pass → lexicon proposes → a purpose-built mark reader decides whether the proposed mark is PRINTED. No HOLD in training labels. No ML restorer or LLM step for now.

The reason: your rule "transcribe what is printed" makes the image the only admissible judge. Every option therefore depends on the same component, a reader that tells which mark is on the vowel. The lexicon's only job is to point the reader at the few vowels worth checking. That lowers the error rate the reader must achieve from about 0.02% to about 1.5% per vowel judged. A restorer trained on normalised text adds proposals the reader would have to reject. In 15 of the 34 cases where Google read the print correctly, it would have "corrected" toward the normalised form.

What the evidence says first

  • The reference normalises. Of the 189 Google-vs-Wikisource differences, 147 are printed as the Wikisource form, 34 as Google's form, 2 as neither, and 6 are unresolved. Real Google mark errors: 149, about 0.9% of mark-bearing vowels.
  • Compound marks dominate the errors. 57 of the 149 (38%) involve breathing + accent on one vowel, although compound marks are only 3.9% of vowels.
  • Base rate. At 0.9% errors per vowel, a reader that judges every vowel needs a false-change rate of ≤ 0.024% to reach 95% precision at 50% recall. Today's shape classifier makes 0.16% (held-out books, τ 0.998). The recogniser, a pixel model trained on Google's labels, makes 0.5%.
  • The mark extractor is the first bottleneck. It credits ascender ink as marks, takes specks and "!" dots for subscripts, misses elision apostrophes, and cuts split letters. In ᾗσι the η prints as two pieces, so the perispomeni sits outside the vowel window and is never counted. The fixes are in Sol run S2 now.

Test plan and go/no-go

  • Instrument: the 1,264 three-way lines with print truth (149 real errors, 34 normalisation traps), held-out books reported separately; the per-slot line metric from S2; your 33 sheet notes as machine-checked expectations; the held-out sheet published after every iteration.
  • Reader go/no-go: false-change rate ≤ 0.1% per judged vowel on held-out agreement vowels, and ≤ 0.05% for a whole-line pass; compound-class recall reported; normalisation traps corrected = 0.
  • Stage go/no-go for training labels: precision against print with an exact 95% lower bound ≥ 95% per stage, on held-out books; otherwise the stage stays proposal-only.
  • Order: S2 extractor fixes (running) → reader trained on agreement labels (Clariden) → combined engine re-scored → owner sheet.
