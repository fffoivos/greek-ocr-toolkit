## Why the live repair was withdrawn

The focused cohort was selected before crop outcomes: all **45 accepted-finite-rule development pages**, all pinned pilots and the named regressions, a 77-page union. Both complete trial routes ran on those 77 native inputs. The final trial changes geometry on 15 pages. `DOMAIN_POPULATIONS.json` enumerates the entire cohort; `REPAIRED_NATIVE.json` and its gallery expose 676 changed old-row comparisons. They are diagnostic masks, not certified glyph classifications, and not all rejected-trial crops were visually adjudicated after a decisive regression was found.

1. **No rule outside its finite interval does not establish one reconnected wide field.** The first implementation fitted the above/below bands. On 4959 p1 this changed the recovery census and lost a residual fragment (accepted #118). The next revision left outside-rule continuation unknown, without mesh/inference. This restored the row but still tightened some lower support. It did not add a crop-success whitelist.
2. **Blank polygon padding across a printed rule is not foreign glyph support.** The first M17 completeness check rejected a near-identical proposal for 4959 p1 #76 when its buffered boundary crossed the rule centre. The correction separated endpoint completeness from clipping at the observed rule and left native M18 retention active. Diagnostic lost dark pixels fell from 4,106 to 115, but that reduction is not a physical pass or tolerance.
3. **Changing valid field observations changes residual requests and their conditional glyph envelopes.** On 4959 p5 accepted final #109, a no-parent residual row, request 1 changes from native y2576–3080 to y2583–3079. The corresponding recovered right-hand row changes from y2702–2757.598 to y2707–2753.734. Native images show cut lower letter/mark support (1,032 diagnostic pixels, with no small-loss waiver). The tighter row is already present in type/residual recovery and survives later stages. No M17 proposal directly represents this particular row. The exact internal scalar contribution remains unresolved; the changed request/census is observed causality, not proof that its seven-pixel shift alone explains the complete effect.

`FAILURE_TRACE.json` binds complete old/new polygons, requests, residual outputs, fields, trial stages and native images to source/input hashes. The three diffs retain the original attempt and both corrections. Some changed masks legitimately lose printed-rule pixels (for example 74cd p7 and 9de6 p5); all lost pixels were not called text. Conversely, visible clipped glyphs were not excused by count or final item agreement. A narrower incoming crop, blind rectangle growth, polygon union, weakened M20 mask or runtime comparison with the old result was not used to hide the failure.

The earliest remaining dependency is the finite-domain change's effect on **residual row/support attribution under a changed census**, plus endpoint-crossing whole components and outside-rule reconnection. A rectangle/polygon domain repair alone cannot certify those. The planner should use the concrete 4959 p5 request/row trace when choosing the next step; this report does not launch or prescribe another iteration.
