# R8-01b — shared observations, incomplete physical scale authority

The running candidate Stack now shares the migrated image, paper, contrast, component and local-extent observations. Equivalent operator bodies have been removed from their callers. One fresh 208-page pass preserves all 15,093 exported items. Two fresh 23-page baseline/candidate timing repeats show small savings, not a speed-target solution.

**G1b is partial.** Connected-ink extent and strip-contact separation have explicit providers, units, support and uncertainty. They are not validated semantic body height on all required controls. Legacy keys such as `body_height` and `xheight` remain in existing field/faintness/type decisions to preserve established behavior while their scheduled replacements are incomplete. The new evidence explicitly identifies those values as hypotheses or engineering scales. This is a real remaining migration debt, not a claim that renaming a value validates it. Ink calibration/ownership is 02; finite domains, row attribution, semantic guides, phase placement and final crops are 03. `ink_normal.follow_letters` remains off-default and is not authoritative. No ownership/removal policy was deleted in this slice.

Different numerical masks remain separate: absolute DN texture contrast in geometry/Q8, closing-paper relative product/division in type, and faintness noise/core photometry. They model different current observations. Merging them merely because they all describe dark pixels would change unsupported physical decisions. Geometry's inherited8% DN floor and Q8's6% DN floor are documented hypotheses, not illumination-invariant front-ink tests. Their calibration remains02/03 work.
