Digital Library


We understand that not everyone can come in person at the Gennadius Library so we try our best to facilitate your research.


As always, you can search our online catalogue AMBROSIA. Our Library staff can scan a limited number of materials on demand. The limit is 50 pages per week, and the material will be delivered within a week.


We remind you of the e-journal titles and online databases that are available to our readers. Do not hesitate to contact us for the links restricted to the ASCSA Domain.


For any of these Library services please contact the Librarian, Irini Solomonidi
