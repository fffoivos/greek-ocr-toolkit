				October 16, 2026
				
The American School mourns the passing on October 12, 2026, of Malcolm Hewitt Wiener.
			
			
 Malcolm Hewitt Wiener, former Chairman of our Board of Trustees. He was 91 and had been continuously and actively involved with the School for 42 years.
			Read More
		
	




	

		
		

			
		
		
		

			
				October 12, 2026
				
“The Place Where I Am From”: A Heartwarming Celebration of Student Achievement and Creativity
			
			
On Saturday, September 26, 2026, the Gennadius Library welcomed students, teachers, families, and friends to the awards ceremony of “The Place Where I Am From” (Ο Τόπος μου), a student competition organized to mark the Library’s centenary and held under the auspices of the Ministry of Education, Religious Affairs and Sports.
			Read More
		
	




	

		
		

			
		
		
		

			
				October 9, 2026
				
Katherine E. Fleming Concludes Thalia Potamianos Lecture Series  Before a Global Audience
			
			
On the evening of October 6, Cotsen Hall was full and humming with anticipation as Katherine E. Fleming rose to deliver the final lecture of the 2025/2026 Thalia Potamianos Lecture Series. With Cotsen Hall at capacity, an overflow audience gathered in the Main Reading Room of the Gennadius Library, while more than 2,000 people joined online, extending the evening well beyond the walls of the American School.
