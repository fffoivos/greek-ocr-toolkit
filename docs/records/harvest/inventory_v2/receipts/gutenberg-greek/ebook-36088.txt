"Νόμοι και Επινομίς, Τόμος Α" by Plato is a philosophical dialogue written in ancient Greece. Plato's longest and final work explores the foundations of law and government through a conversation between an Athenian stranger and two companions journeying to Zeus's cave. Tasked with creating laws for a new colony, the three men debate divine versus human origins of law, the role of the soul, education, and justice. Notably, this is Plato's only
