"Νόμοι και Επινομίς, Τόμος Δ" by Plato is a philosophical dialogue written in ancient Greece as Plato's final work. Three elderly men journey to Zeus's sacred cave, discussing who deserves credit for creating laws. When one reveals he must establish laws for a new colony, they spend their trek designing an entire legal system for the imagined city of Magnesia. Through twelve dense books, they explore divine law, government ethics, education, and
