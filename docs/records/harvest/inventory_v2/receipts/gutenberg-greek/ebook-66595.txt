"Monumentum Ancyranum: The Deeds of Augustus" by Emperor of Rome Augustus is a monumental inscription completed in AD 14. Written in first person, Augustus chronicles his political career, public generosity, military conquests, and honors received from the Roman people. This carefully crafted self-portrait presents the emperor's version of history, where enemies remain nameless and accomplishments shine brightest. Originally engraved on bronze pillars before his mausoleum, copies spread throughout the empire, offering insight
