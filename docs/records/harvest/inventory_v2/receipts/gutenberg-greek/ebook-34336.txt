"Ο Πατούχας" by Ioannes Kondylakes is a novel first published in serialized form in 1892. Set in mid-19th century Turkish-occupied Crete, it follows Manolis, a primitive eighteen-year-old who suddenly descends from years of isolation as a shepherd in the mountains to enter village society. His wild behavior, ignorance of social conventions, and unrestrained impulses create chaos as he navigates love, rejection, and community life.
