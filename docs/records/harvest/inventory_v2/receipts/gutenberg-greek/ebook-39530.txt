"Πολιτεία, Τόμος 4" by Plato is a philosophical dialogue written around 380 BCE. In this work, Socrates and prominent Athenians debate the meaning of justice and whether a just person is happier than an unjust one. Through their discussion, they construct an ideal city called "Kallipolis," ruled by philosopher-kings. The dialogue explores the nature of the philosopher, Plato's Theory of Forms including the famous Allegory of the Cave, the relationship between philosophy
