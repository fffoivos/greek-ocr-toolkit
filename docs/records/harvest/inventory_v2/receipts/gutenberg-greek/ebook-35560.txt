"Quo Vadis (Πού πηγαίνεις): Μυθιστόρημα της Νερωνικής Εποχής" by Henryk Sienkiewicz is a historical novel written in 1895-1896. Set in Rome during Nero's reign from AD 64 to 68, it follows the forbidden romance between Marcus Vinicius, a Roman patrician, and Lygia, a Christian woman and barbarian hostage. As their love deepens, Vinicius must confront his pagan world's brutality and the growing persecution of Christians, culminating in Nero's violent scapegoating after Rome's
