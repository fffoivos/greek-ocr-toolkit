The book explores themes of heroism and tragedy, centering around the character Μήτρος Ρουμελιώτης, a young man who embodies the valiant spirit of his community. At the start of the novel, the atmosphere is set during the somber night of Μεγάλη Παρασκευή (Great Friday), when the local populace engages in preparations for the mourning rituals associated with the

This incident sets off a chain of events that unfolds amid communal anxieties about his injury and its implications, showcasing both the tight-knit community dynamics and the heavier themes of mortality, masculinity, and the societal expectations surrounding the concept of beauty and strength.
