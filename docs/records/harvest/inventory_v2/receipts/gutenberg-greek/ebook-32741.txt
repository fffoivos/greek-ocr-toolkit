"Ποιήματα - Τόμος Πέμπτος - Φασουλής Φιλόσοφος" by Georgios Souris is a collection of poems written in the late 19th century. The work explores philosophical themes through a satirical lens, reflecting on human existence, desire, and the futility of life. The opening of this collection introduces the reader to the

Through rich imagery, the verses convey the essence of being human amidst absurdity and chaos, as Φασουλής grapples with his identity and the world around him.
