"Τίμαιος, Τόμος Β" by Plato is a philosophical dialogue written around 360 BC. Through long monologues by the character Timaeus, the work explores the nature of the physical world and human existence. Timaeus describes how a divine craftsman—the demiurge—created the universe by bringing order to chaos, using eternal and perfect forms as a template.
