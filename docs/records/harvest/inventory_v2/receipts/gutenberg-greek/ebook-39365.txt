This work explores the origins, influences, and implications of Socrates' thought, along with his methodologies and philosophical contributions. The author delves into the complexities of Socratic teachings and their enduring impact on subsequent philosophical traditions, particularly in relation to Plato and Aristotle. The opening of this






text sets the stage for a rigorous analysis of Socratic philosophy, indicating the importance of understanding Socrates not only as a historical figure but as a profound thinker whose ideas laid the groundwork for Western philosophy. Vrailas-Armenes discusses the methodological approaches to studying Socratic thought, emphasizing the necessity of examining various historical sources, such as the works of Xenophon and Plato, to capture the essence of Socratic teachings.
