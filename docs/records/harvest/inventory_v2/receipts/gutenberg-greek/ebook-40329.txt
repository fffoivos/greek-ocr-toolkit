"Ο Γερμανικός Φιλελληνισμός" by Paulos Karolides is a historical account written in the early 20th century. The work primarily discusses the role of German philhellenism in supporting the Greek War of Independence, emphasizing its philosophical, cultural, and moral implications. The author argues that the German support for Greece during this tumultuous period was substantial and unique, highlighting its impact on the success of the Greek struggle for freedom. The opening of the






text presents the author's motivations for writing this treatise, which stem from a lecture given at the Society of Friends of the People regarding the historical context of the Greek struggle. Karolides notes that many listeners were unaware of the significant contributions of German philhellenism, as the discourse often emphasizes the roles of other European powers. He expresses a desire to clarify this historical aspect, positing that understanding the past is essential for future direction. The author stresses that the implications of German philhellenism transcend mere sympathy for the Greek cause, advocating for a nuanced exploration of its ethical and political dimensions in facilitating Greece's pursuit of liberation.
