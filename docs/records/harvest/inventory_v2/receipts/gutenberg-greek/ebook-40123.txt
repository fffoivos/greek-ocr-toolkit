"Οι ευτράπελες ιστορίες του, Νασρ-εν-ντιν Χότζα" by K. Trikoglides is a collection of humorous stories written in the early 20th century. The book centers around the character of Nāsr al-Dīn Ḥoṭza, a beloved figure in Eastern folklore known for his cleverness and wit, as he navigates various amusing situations and shares insights about human nature and society. The opening of this work introduces us to the legendary character of Nāsr al-Dīn Ḥoṭza,






who is greatly appreciated among the Eastern peoples for his tales and anecdotes that encapsulate folk wisdom. The first tale presents an amusing scenario where a peasant brings a wild goose as a gift to the clever, yet comical, Ḥoṭza, which leads to humorous consequences involving unexpected guests and the clever use of language.
