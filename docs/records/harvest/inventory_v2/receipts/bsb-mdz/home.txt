From the MDZ

      

        



        
        






The Munich DigitiZation Center (MDZ) has been providing the rich holdings of the Bavarian State Library (BSB) on the Internet since 1997. 





It is the 

central innovation and also production unit of the BSB

 for developing, testing and implementing new products and processes related to the multifaceted, far-ranging subject of the 'digital library'.

        
      
      
        

          

            

              07/24/2026
            
            

              

                


Valuable 15th-century french chronicle digitized
                
                






The valuable French parchment manuscript 'Chroniques de France (commonly known as de Sainct-Denis), tome 1', catalogued as BSB Cod.gall. 4, was digitized at the ScanningCenter. This chronicle of French history dates from the 15th century and contains numerous miniatures.





To the chronicle in the Digital Collections

13th-century manuscript digitized
                
                






The valuable 13th-century manuscript ‘Ordo divini officii cum constitutionibus canoniae S. Nicol. (in fine mancus)’ (BSB Clm 16104 a) was digitized at the ScanningCenter.





To the Manuscript in the Digital Collections

                
              
              

                

                  






                  
                
              
            
          
        
        

          

            

              07/24/2026
            
            

              

                


Artist's Book by Henri Matisse (1869–1954)
                
                






The work 'Jazz’ by Henri Matisse has been digitized at the ScanningCenter. The valuable artist’s book was published in 1947. It features color paper-cut illustrations as well as accompanying texts by the artist and is considered one of the most significant works of book art of the 20th century.





To the work in the Digital Collections
