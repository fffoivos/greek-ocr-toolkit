# Rights inventory

Rights are recorded, not decided. A source declaration is not a copyright determination or an acquisition approval. Each item needs separate scan, underlying-text and access records. Cloud OCR, training, retention and redistribution are not inferred from free reading, a metadata licence, an old owner-admission note, a date, or a public-domain label in another jurisdiction.

Where marked **owner decision**, the owner must decide whether to rely on Greek Law 2121/1993 Art. 21A/21B, which entity and access qualify, rights reservations, secure retention and any processor terms. This inventory does not assert that an exception applies. The requested legal framework is recorded in the [plan-input receipt](receipts/local/input-files.json); no legal eligibility finding is made.

Short verbatim excerpts appear below; complete retrieved wording is retained in the linked HTTP body/terms receipt. Missing or blocked wording is explicitly unresolved. No permissions, forms, orders or gated-dataset terms were accepted.
