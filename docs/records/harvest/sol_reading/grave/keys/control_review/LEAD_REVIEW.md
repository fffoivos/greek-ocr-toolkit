# Lead pixel review: grave/acute bed (2026-10-17 13:34 Athens)

The Opus lead checked all five SR-G1 contact sheets by eye (letter image 8x plus word image 3x).

- **Positives, 26/26 printed grave.** καθὼς is the least clear: a short teardrop stroke, but it falls to the right. ποτὲ and Καθαρὸ are faint but directional.
- **Negatives, 26/26 printed acute.**
- **Controls, 6/6 valid:**
  - circumflex: βροῦμε, ποῦ, τῆς;
  - none on the final vowel: προηγηθέντα, Παιδείας, νάθελε. The Παιδείας letter zoom also shows the accented ί beside the anchor α.

**Confound to report.** Many negatives touch punctuation or a line-end hyphen (Γιατρέ., διδαχή!, παρά-, σκληρά,). This era's convention keeps the acute exactly there, and puts a grave on a final acute before another word. Accuracy is therefore also reported separately for words not touching punctuation or a hyphen.
