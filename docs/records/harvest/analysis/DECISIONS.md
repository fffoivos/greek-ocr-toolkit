## 10. Implementation notes and deviations from the brief

- R2: width_ratio > 0.8 on pages detected as 1-column only for the six all-pages vouli books (see rules.json R2 notes). Page majority uses pages with ≥ 1 admitted line.
- R3: low/high side of C:cpp_vs_book_out read from the flag reason ('<' = low); all-caps from B is_all_caps.
- R4: C:repeated_word compares tokens with punctuation; the extra exact-pair check also keeps punctuation (so 'μέτρα, μέτρα' is not a doubled reading); the accent-variant check strips punctuation and treats grave = acute (ποτέ / ποτὲ is a positional variant, not a second reading).
- R5: narrowed from `\S- \S` (see rules.json).
- R6: the spacing tonos U+0384 is only a tonos in monotonic lines; in polytonic lines it stands for a breathing and those lines are dropped as ambiguous. Spacing marks before Υ/Ρ with no precomposed capital (psili on Υ, psili on Ρ) are left unchanged.
- R7: single-letter matches only for the articles Ο/Η (Α, Ι … are figure letters in geometry/physics books); a bare capital at line end (vote mark) and all-caps lines are ignored.
- R8(b) (plain σ for στ) is a RELABEL, not a drop: every sampled case is 'σρατ-', 'κατασρέφ-', 'ἐπέσρεψ-' with an attested στ form.
- R9: the 'Cyrillic in non-Cyrillic text' half excludes the cyrillic wave and Cyrillic-majority books, whose Latin-class lines legitimately contain Bulgarian/Russian words.
- R10: when one copy of an identical page has 0 admitted lines nothing is dropped (keeping the empty copy would only delete the other).
- R11 is metadata only: the clean preview keeps `year` and adds 'R11' to `rules_applied` for every OpenArchives line; consumers should treat year as null and the title as unreliable. OpenArchives dossiers whose notes say the catalogue title/year does not match the content: 020, 023, 039, 040, 043, 055, 058, 059, 064, 069, 073, 075, 079, 090, 123, 124, 128, 129, 130, 132, 135, 139 (22).
- R13: no minimum page size (as briefed); 5 lines come from pages with fewer than 5 admitted lines.
- R14: leader runs apply pool-wide (they also catch ellipses written as ≥ 5 dots and table-of-contents leaders); the trailing vote mark only in vouli.
- R15: predicates are the adjudicators' rules as written, except 084 (split-word) and 113 (folded into R7). 'C:cpp_vs_book_out' in a book rule means either side, since the adjudicators did not restrict it.
