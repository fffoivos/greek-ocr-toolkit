## 1. Method: per-aspect score, hard-flag weights, suspicion

Per book and aspect, **score** = number of the aspect's z-scored features with |z| > 2 against books of the same majority script class, using the z-scores the aspect wrote (A: `zscores`; B: `z_median` + `z_flag_share`; C: `z`, which includes flag/OOV shares; D: `z`). **suspicion** = (number of aspects with any |z| > 2) + Σ hard-flag weights. Ranking: suspicion, then n_lines (both descending).

| aspect | hard flag | weight | why |
|---|---|---:|---|
| A | other_script_residue | 1; **0** when every residue line is of an explained kind (`explained_only`) | A §7: letter+digit tokens and number-adjacent reference letters are usually correct |
| A | greek_book_other_lang_gt2pct | 1 | spec flag |
| A | book_other_lang_gt2pct (non-Greek books) | 1; **0** when ≥ 80 % of the other-language lines are ru/bg/sr in a Slavic-majority book | A key finding 2: ru/bg confusion of the function-word detector |
| A | poly_mark_density_low | 0.5 | A §7: class-p5 rule, ~5 % of polytonic books flagged by construction |
| B | final_sigma_above_class_p95 | 1 | needs ≥ 2 occurrences; a label-error signal |
| B | all_caps_above_class_p95 | 0.5 | class-p95 rule; B §6: all-caps describes content type, not label quality |
| B | below_support | 0 | single occurrence (instruction) |
| C | hard_conf, hard_oov_pool, hard_oov_wikisource | 0.5 each | C Limits: class p5/p95, ~5 % of books by construction |
| C | hard_cpp | 0 | C finding 1: raw chars/100 px measures scan resolution; all 3 books are inside range on the resolution-invariant measure (hard_cph_extra = 0) |
| D | column_crossing, column_merge, duplicate_scan, shares_text_with_other_book | 1 each | D §9 |
| D | page_line_count_outlier | 0 | D §9: wave p1/p99, ~1 % of pages per side by construction (82 books) |

Suspicion distribution (books): 6.5: 4, 6: 5, 5.5: 7, 5: 13, 4.5: 14, 4: 38, 3.5: 14, 3: 49, 2.5: 24, 2: 100, 1.5: 14, 1: 146, 0.5: 4, 0: 125.

Per-line "anomaly count" (used to pick the 20 most anomalous lines per dossier) = number of line flags across A–D, excluding D `page:lines_above_wave_p99` / `page:lines_below_wave_p1` (page-level, by construction), C `cpp_out_of_range` (scan resolution) and A residue of the explained kinds (`*_alnum`, `*_ref_letter`); ties broken by the number of label-quality flags (list in §2.5), then seeded random.

## 3. Cross-cutting findings (where the reports agree) and the rule that fixes each

LINE-LEVEL = an automatic rule on the line label/geometry, applicable to the whole pool without review. BOOK-LEVEL = needs a per-book decision (adjudication).

### 3.1 Library stamps admitted as text — LINE-LEVEL (drop)

- B: 83 `digitisation_stamp` lines in 18 books (round1 University of Crete seal, Wikisource `Digitized by Google`, `ΒΙΒΛΙΟΘΗΚΗ`); C: 74 of the 96 `height_ratio_high` lines are the same seal (C finding 4); D excludes the seal from its column geometry (D §9). **44 are in test and 32 in dev** (train 7).
- Rule: drop a line whose whole label matches `^[-–\s]*(ΠΑΝΕΠΙΣΤΗΜΙΟ?Ν?(\s+ΚΡ\w*)?|ΒΙΒΛΙΟΘΗΚΗ|Digitized by Google)\W*$` (B's detector). Do **not** use a bare prefix `^ΠΑΝΕΠΙΣΤΗΜΙ`: it also hits ≈ 30 other lines, mostly genuine OpenArchives title-page lines (`ΠΑΝΕΠΙΣΤΗΜΙΟ ΑΙΓΑΙΟΥ`, `ΠΑΝΕΠΙΣΤΗΜΙΟ ΘΕΣΣΑΛΙΑΣ …`). Geometric backstop: round1 boxes with height ratio > 1.7 at y > 0.85 of the page.

### 3.2 Column merges (two-column pages read as one line) in Vouli and Cyrillic — LINE-LEVEL (drop) + BOOK-LEVEL for fully merged books

- D: 3,591 `column_merge_line` lines (vouli 2,575, cyrillic 502, mixed 225, parliament 106, round1 66, openarchives 46, wikisource 39, round2 32); hard flag in 16 books (15 vouli + archive_AA9613308335D0B014B 9a32fb66), column_crossing in 19. C independently finds the ≈ 2× length mode in vouli (C finding 2; `len_vs_book_high` 1,232 vouli lines) — 989 of D's merge lines are also C `aspect_out`, 759 also `len_vs_book_high`. Split: train 2,946, dev 278, test 367.
- Rule (LINE-LEVEL): drop lines with D `column_merge_line` (width > 0.8 × page text width AND left- and right-column lines above and below it); additionally drop vouli lines with `len_vs_book` > 1.7 on pages with ≥ 2 detected columns. A geometric split of the crop is possible at the detected gutter x, but the label cannot be split reliably (no per-word boxes in the pool), so drop rather than split — unless the Google word boxes are recovered from records, in which case split label and crop at the gutter.
- BOOK-LEVEL: pages where Google merged *every* line escape the detector (D §9 limit i: no narrow lines → seen as one column). The 16 column_merge books and the vouli books with crossing share > 0.5 are in the dossiers; an adjudicator should decide DROP_BOOK where the sheet shows merges on most lines.

### 3.3 Breathing/accent on a capital written two ways — LINE-LEVEL (label normalisation)

- B: 9,789 lines write a spacing mark before a bare capital (`᾿Αθῆναι`) while 29,226 lines use the precomposed capital (`Ἀθῆναι`); 359 of the 384 books with the spacing form also use the precomposed form — the same printed glyph gets two targets (B finding 1). A notes the consequence: spacing breathings are not counted as marks, lowering mark density in caps-heavy lines (A §7). By wave: round1 3,948, round2 1,652, parliament 1,443, vouli 1,142, mixed 1,138, wikisource 464, openarchives 2.
- Rule: for a spacing mark at word start (or after `(`, `«`, quote) followed by a Greek capital, delete the spacing mark and attach the combining equivalent to the capital, then NFC: `᾿`U+1FBF→U+0313, `῾`U+1FFE→U+0314, `῎`U+1FCE→U+0313 U+0301, `῍`U+1FCD→U+0313 U+0300, `῏`U+1FCF→U+0313 U+0342, `῞`U+1FDE→U+0314 U+0301, `῝`U+1FDD→U+0314 U+0300, `῟`U+1FDF→U+0314 U+0342, `΄`U+0384 → U+0301 (`᾿Α`→`Ἀ`, `῞Ε`→`Ἕ`). Keep elision/coronis after a letter (`ἀλλ᾿`, `κ᾿`) and aphaeresis `᾿ς` unchanged. Apply to labels at load time (train and eval).

### 3.4 στ ligature read as final ς (and other final-sigma errors) — LINE-LEVEL (relabel / drop)

- B: 452 `final_sigma_error` lines; word-internal ς 215 occurrences, of which 100 have the στ spelling attested elsewhere in the pool (`μέγιςος`, `Πανεπιςημίου`, `καταςροφὴν`) — by line: 98 lines in 45 books (vouli 49, round1 28, round2 12, mixed 5, wikisource 3, parliament 1); 245 word-final σ. Hard flag in 24 books. Matches the measured DocAI behaviour of never emitting ϛ (a look-alike is substituted).
- Rule: (a) RELABEL `ς` followed by a Greek letter → `στ` when the resulting word is attested in the pool (exact NFC-lowercase match), excluding the compound-boundary cases B lists (προς-, εις-, ὡς-, -δήποτε, ὥςτε, aphaeresis `᾿ς`) which follow the print; (b) remaining word-internal `ς` before a letter, and word-final `σ` not followed by `.` (abbreviation), → drop the line. Whether the ligature glyph should be labelled `στ` (text) or `ϛ` (glyph) is a labelling-policy choice for the owner; the pool should be consistent either way.

### 3.5 Wrong-script whole words (homoglyph reads) — LINE-LEVEL (drop)

- A: 344 unexplained residue lines in 115 books — kinds unexpected_standalone 160, isolated_standalone 67, unexpected_standalone_lookalike 65, unexpected_word 35, isolated_standalone_lookalike 15, other_script 2; 48 lines carry Cyrillic letters inside Greek/Latin text (`ВЕ`, `ОПР`, `По`, `сатра-`) and `A-` for `Ἀ-` type Latin capitals (A finding 1). Soft `homoglyph_word` adds 230 lines (Latin `Ha`/`ce` in Cyrillic lines, `TOD` in Greek lines). Admission cannot see these because each foreign token is a whole word in one script.
- Rule: drop the line when it contains a token of ≥ 1 letter whose script differs from the line's majority script AND every letter of the token has a look-alike in the majority script (A's `*_standalone_lookalike`, `unexpected_word`, `homoglyph_word` with no genuine foreign word on the line), or any Cyrillic letter in a non-Cyrillic book. Do not drop `*_alnum` / `*_ref_letter` (formulae, Stephanus pages `591 B`) or acronyms on an allowlist (IBM, NATO, …). Relabelling by homoglyph map (Latin A→Greek Α) is possible but only safe for single capitals; dropping costs ≈ 0.1 % of lines. The 107 hard-flag books without a dossier are almost all covered by this rule.

### 3.6 Foreign-language lines in bilingual books — BOOK-LEVEL (no automatic drop)

- A: 3,243 lines differ from their book's language (mixed wave exempt), 2,587 of them Latin-script lines in Greek-majority books; 55 Greek books exceed 2 % (top: bilingual editions at 25–41 %), 14 non-Greek books (mostly Cyrillic ru/bg detector noise). B/C see the same books as high Latin share and high OOV; split: train 2,826, dev 93, test 324.
- These lines are genuine text and are already routed by their line `script_class` (latin / greek_latin_cs), so no label rule applies. Rule: keep; the BOOK-LEVEL question for the adjudicator is only whether the Latin-script labels in that book are right (accents, ligatures, Fraktur) — DROP_PATTERN "Latin-script lines" where they are not. For the Greek-only eval report, compute metrics per script_class so bilingual books do not mix in Latin CER.

### 3.7 Duplicate scans across Vouli volume keys — LINE-LEVEL (dedupe) + BOOK-LEVEL check

- D: 7 byte-identical page images under different page ids (all vouli), 3 more page pairs with label Jaccard ≥ 0.6; 13 books `duplicate_scan`, 9 `shares_text_with_other_book` (pairs share 181, 33, 29, 29, 27 lines, then ≤ 4) — 15 books, 940 duplicated lines in them (vouli 927). No label crosses train ↔ dev/test (D §0.2); 3 identical images across splits were already emptied by the admission's holdout-label filter. A sees the same sessions as low-mark-density/high-tonos books (the duplicates are often the monotonic re-typeset copy).
- Rule: group page images by `image_sha256` and books by shared ≥ 25-char suspicious labels (≥ 10 shared); keep the copy in dev/test if the split differs, else the one with more admitted lines; drop the duplicated lines from the other copy. BOOK-LEVEL: 6db81fc97db3 ↔ 92d580f394fe (181 shared lines) look like the same session under two keys — drop one key entirely if the adjudicator confirms.

### 3.8 OpenArchives year metadata — BOOK-LEVEL (metadata fix, not a label issue)

- A: 13 OpenArchives items carry a year < 1950 but a monotonic majority (e.g. an NB-IoT thesis dated 1916; A §5) — 3,909 lines; 1 OA item has no year. OA years by decade: 0s 1, 1880s 1, 1890s 1, 1910s 1, 1920s 2, 1930s 6, 1940s 2, 1950s 4, 1960s 4, 1970s 3, 1980s 5, 1990s 3, 2000s 25, 2010s 43, 2020s 22.
- Rule: treat OA `year` as unreliable: set `year = null` when year < 1950 and the book is greek_mono-majority (monotonic spelling was official only from 1982), and stratify OA by script class, not by decade. Labels are unaffected. Books: openarchives_Dione|a181595d (1927); openarchives_Dione|c82ac45f (1930); openarchives_KEIED|7f5d5d2e (1948); openarchives_KEIED|94fbbe22 (1936); openarchives_KEIED|a0822ede (1948); openarchives_KEIED|a4e7bf54 (1937); openarchives_Pandemos|a40fcf11 (1937); openarchives_hellanicus|2f4f1c7f (1881); openarchives_hellanicus|ca84e046 (1932); openarchives_mixed_KEIED|18655fc2 (1936); openarchives_ntua|46380632 (1890); openarchives_ntua|53517087 (1922); openarchives_ntua|fc3264cd (1916).

### 3.9 Doubled readings — LINE-LEVEL (drop)

- C: 417 lines with two identical adjacent words (≥ 3 letters) in 194 books; some genuine (κάτω κάτω, λίγο λίγο, trinomials), many Google reading the text twice (C finding 3). Aggregator check: 69 lines have ≥ 2 adjacent doubled pairs and 209 lines have an adjacent pair that differs only in accents (a polytonic and a monotonic reading interleaved, e.g. «τοῦ Συντονισμοῦ στὴν Στατιστικὴ Ὑπηρεσία, Υπηρεσία, ἔχει ἔχει σχέση σχέση»). D adds 1,037 lines repeated at another position on the same page (mostly vote lists and table cells; D §9) — expected, no rule.
- Rule: drop a line with ≥ 2 adjacent doubled pairs, or with any adjacent pair that is equal after removing diacritics but differs in its accents (case-only differences such as «Ὄχι, ὄχι» excluded); keep a single exact repeat (genuine reduplication) unless it is also a C `len_vs_book_high` / `cpp_vs_book_out` outlier.
