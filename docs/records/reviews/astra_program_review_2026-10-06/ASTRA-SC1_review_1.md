# ASTRA-SC1 — Polytonic spell-check / accent-detection review

**Verdict: FAIL for acceptance as an independent, print-faithful accent fact-checker.** The track has useful, tested improvements and mostly keeps reference text out of the current reading path. However, the default transcription deletes marks for orthographic reasons, several evaluation instruments cannot establish the claimed physical coverage, and reproducible ownership and reporting defects remain. This verdict does not mean that the recorded improvements are all invalid or that R1 should be discarded.

Scope: source and saved-artifact review, 68 targeted tests, synthetic counterexamples, and six existing S6 fixture crops. Tests and spot checks together used **11 distinct native line crops**. ## Question 1 — Toolkit reuse and recommended substitutions

**No, the track has not used all relevant existing toolkit geometry.** It directly reuses `glyphs.components.line_glyphs`, then changes its band and replaces the toolkit's letter construction with its own CTC-driven regions. The only direct glyph/page imports found in `greekocr/labelfix` and `scripts/labelfix` are the component extraction in `ink.py`; the other facilities listed below are not consumed there. R1 deliberately has a separate pixel implementation: its brief explicitly prohibited `ink.py`/x-height-model dependence and superseded the earlier connected-component admission test (`docs/records/briefs/R1_mark_reader_scripts.md:14–15,24–41`). That isolation was an instructed experimental design, not an accidental failure to import a library.

### How strong is the toolkit's evidence?

The toolkit letter implementation encodes the owner rule and has explicit physical tests; its docstring records a 16,540-pair rendered column-overlap check (`greekocr/glyphs/letters.py:3–12`). The guide implementation has skew/bend, four-guide and straightening tests (`tests/glyphs/test_glyphs.py:242–255,306–320,392–399`). Straightening has an OCR calibration, including its caveats: the margin was selected on the same 40 texts, its confidence interval permits regression, and E1 cut/skew limits were not established (`docs/GLYPHS_CALIBRATION.md:108–139`). Page capital-heading tests are evidence of specific behavior, not general mark-ownership validation.

A blind switch back to toolkit defaults is not justified. In the six native S6 crops, `line_glyphs` gives x-height **48 versus S6's 31** for `602cc72e-p000155-l0009`, and **40 versus 31** for `602cc72e-p000113-l0030`; the latter's inspected overlay places S6's band nearer the visible small-letter body. Toolkit rough-letter counts exceed recogniser-letter counts on four of the six examples (25/23, 18/17, 35/33, 43/42). These discrepancies do not by themselves identify which segmentation is right. The recommendation is to consolidate physical contracts and preserve tested repairs, not to assert that every toolkit estimate is better.

## Ranked findings

### B01 — BLOCKER: default orthographic deletion violates the printed-text contract

**Evidence.** `greekocr/labelfix/reading.py:65–69` enables every rule except C1-after-consonant and C3; `151–173` deletes an enabled proposal, and `242–253` proposes C4 deletion solely from syllable/letter legality. `265,329,332–346` makes that behavior the default transcription API. For singleton C4 violations, the recorded “keep” alternative is never used as a decision gate. The synthetic probe supplies a circumflex on epsilon with probability 1.0 and a 50-pixel mark; default filtering changes `ε͂` to `ε`. A printed misprint of this shape cannot survive the rule regardless of its ink support.

The existing cohort already contradicts the premise that orthographic illegality establishes absent ink: C1-after-consonant proposes deleting a printed breathing in `Ναὕρουν`, and C3 proposes 16 printed accent removals (`docs/records/labelfix/spellcheck/K1_REPORT.md:27–39` and its printed-conflict table). Holding those two rules back is correct, but does not establish that the remaining universal rules preserve every edition's misprints. K1 still deletes 10 unknown held-out marks and 3 unknown DEV marks; six enabled subrules had no removal proposals at all (`docs/records/labelfix/spellcheck/K1_REPORT.md:5,21,25–39`). Zero proposals are no empirical precision qualification.

**Fix.** Make print transcription preserve the pixel reading by default. Use orthographic rules to flag suspicious output and trigger an evidence-based reread, not to delete a supported mark. Keep normalized/corrected Greek as a separate, explicitly selected output. Maintain C1/C3 holdbacks; add printed-illegal-mark fixtures. If a deletion policy remains, require independent visual evidence of absence/wrong ownership and abstain on unknown/cut-off evidence. Do not describe the 13 unknown deletions as established corrections.

### M01 — MAJOR: “print truth” and independence claims require separate supervision, agreement and untouched-test strata

**Evidence.** R1 training labels require exact Google=recogniser NFD core equality (`scripts/labelfix/clariden/r1_common.py:89–97`; `scripts/labelfix/clariden/r1_extract.py:46,60–64`). C1 first selects all Google-labelled compound lines plus 30,000 other lines (`scripts/labelfix/clariden/c1_select.py:59–71`). The recogniser's Google-derived supervision is acknowledged in `greekocr/labelfix/validate.py:5–8` and `docs/records/briefs/R0_recogniser_audit.md:9–11`. Agreement with that recogniser is therefore correlated pseudo-supervision, not independent verification of the printed mark. A shared omission is accepted as a negative label.

Evaluation also mixes sources: `greekocr/labelfix/linemetric.py:16–29` accepts triple agreement unless explicitly adjudicated, while `docs/records/labelfix/spellcheck/S2_REPORT.md:5` identifies only 189 overriding adjudications and discloses historical held-out calibration by S1. Capital truth is only Google/recogniser base agreement, not blind case truth (`scripts/labelfix/s5_report.py:24–36`); runtime case uses the recogniser prior (`greekocr/labelfix/case.py:75–81`). The 96.1%/98.6% combined S6 capital P/R is consequently agreement-conditioned, while pixel-only recall is 46.9% (`docs/records/labelfix/spellcheck/S6_REPORT.md:37–38`). It cannot adjudicate who is right in the 753 pixel/recogniser disagreements.

K1 further selects the rule allowlist on **all** saved S6 lines before reporting held-out scores: `scripts/labelfix/k1_validate.py:202–217` repeatedly removes rules with printed conflicts on either split. Its report correctly calls this cohort qualification (`docs/records/labelfix/spellcheck/K1_REPORT.md:21`), but “held-out” is no longer an untouched generalisation claim for K1.

**Fix.** Record truth provenance per character/slot: blind print adjudication, agreement proxy, owner inference, or unknown. Report each separately. Audit agreement-label noise by mark class, especially systematic Google omissions, and use independent print labels or qualified synthetic marks to cover shared mistakes. Move rule/model choices to development books and reserve fresh books for the final locked evaluation. Finish the independent base/case/span audit described by R0 before claiming that the combined case system corrects the recogniser.

**Boundary finding.** I found no direct Google/human/print-truth argument in the active `read_ink` path or R1 evaluation input construction (`greekocr/labelfix/reading.py:265–329`; `scripts/labelfix/clariden/r1_prepare.py:48–55`; `scripts/labelfix/clariden/r1_extract.py:35–46`). R1's stored disjointness receipt reports zero intersections with all seven declared exclusion sets. The code rejects nonzero intersections and uses a 300/16 document split (`scripts/labelfix/clariden/r1_common.py:46–65`; `scripts/labelfix/clariden/r1_extract.py:110–123`). No actual train/held-out ID overlap was demonstrated. This review did not independently reread/re-hash the entire source corpus or establish cross-edition/image deduplication. S4 intentionally includes DEV samples in fitting and retains a line-level DEV calibration partition (`scripts/labelfix/s4_train.py:90–117`); DEV is not a clean test set.

### M03 — MAJOR: S6 converts unsupported printed mark sets into known “no marks”

**Evidence.** `scripts/labelfix/s6_validate.py:58–62` calls `mark_set(mark_class(b.marks))` without checking for `None`. `greekocr/labelfix/markshape.py:111–129` returns `None` for unsupported marks or multiple accents, and `greekocr/labelfix/linemetric.py:48–51` maps `None` to the empty set. This differs from the older scorer, which explicitly preserves unknown at `greekocr/labelfix/linemetric.py:91–92`.

**Reproduction.** The probe supplies adjudicated printed `ά̀` against a recognised alpha. `char_truth` returns `{0: []}`: a printed two-accent misprint becomes known unmarked truth. This can reward deletion and label real marks as false. No prevalence claim is made for the saved corpus.

**Fix.** Preserve `None`/unsupported truth and record the reason, or extend the truth representation to retain arbitrary printed combining marks and multiplicity. Add fixtures for double accents, macron/breve and unsupported marks. Recompute affected character metrics and K1 qualification only after the truth conversion is repaired.

### M04 — MAJOR: clipping can replace a mark with another component's ink

**Evidence.** `greekocr/labelfix/ink.py:409–421` says it preserves the native label, but actually picks the most populous nonzero label in the blob's **bounding rectangle**, then rebuilds the clipped blob from that label. `Blob` has no parent ID/mask (`greekocr/labelfix/ink.py:53–68,86–90`). Whole-line duplicate suppression independently infers parent identity from all labels in the rectangle (`greekocr/labelfix/reading.py:285–300`). A bounding rectangle is not the blob's membership mask.

**Reproduction.** A 32-pixel parent-1 blob surrounds a separate 48-pixel parent-2 component. Clipping parent 1 changes bbox `[2,2,14,12]` to `[4,4,12,10]` and returns **48 pixels of parent 2 only**. A clipping operation has increased area and substituted foreign ink. The existing test that native page labels remain unchanged cannot catch this evidence substitution.

**Fix.** Carry exact source component IDs and the local connected-subcomponent mask with each blob. Intersect that original mask with the per-x legal zone; never rediscover membership by majority label. Deduplicate by source pixels/identity, not inferred rectangle majority. Assert clipped pixels are a subset of original pixels, area never grows, and union ownership is conserved.

### M05 — MAJOR: case features omit part of a disconnected-stroke union

**Evidence.** `_region` correctly finds pixels from every parent ID but stores only `index=ids[0]` (`greekocr/labelfix/segmentation.py:14–24`). `case.features` and its upper/lower-width checks then use `labels == c.index` (`greekocr/labelfix/case.py:20–36,63–74`). `case.prepare` explicitly reclassifies split unions using that incomplete virtual component (`144–148`). Thus the region dimensions include all strokes while occupancy, holes, thickness and shape grid can describe only one stroke.

**Reproduction.** A two-parent letter region has actual ink occupancy 2/3; case features report 1/3. This is a code-level counterexample, not a claim that every split eta is misclassified.

**Fix.** Give virtual regions a mask or complete parent-label set and make all case/shape consumers use it within the region bounds. Re-evaluate capitals, ascenders, split eta and capital side-mark decisions on independent pixel truth. Reusing `LetterBox.components` would supply the missing ownership contract (`greekocr/glyphs/letters.py:32–41`; `greekocr/glyphs/evaluate.py:62–66`).

### M06 — MAJOR: S6's physical certification exceeds its geometry evidence

**Evidence.** Shared native components are partitioned whenever multiple cached characters choose them; there is no physical split acceptance requirement (`greekocr/labelfix/segmentation.py:59–83`). A narrow shared component is flagged `segmentation_uncertain`, but `greekocr/labelfix/reading.py:273–277` still calls the interval `certified_body`, and `greekocr/labelfix/ink.py:279–283` records it as one letter. `localisation_unknown` fallback regions are also searched and can emit ordinary parts (`greekocr/labelfix/segmentation.py:98–107`; `greekocr/labelfix/reading.py:311–320`). Flags are useful, but “one region per recogniser character” is not proof that the printed letters occupy those regions.

Separately, S6 replaces potentially curved guides with a straight baseline (`greekocr/labelfix/case.py:104–115`) and a single line x-height for normal words (`138–143`). Fused splitting uses a horizontal body slice at the component centre (`greekocr/labelfix/segmentation.py:65–67`); attached marks and final clipping likewise use scalar heights (`greekocr/labelfix/ink.py:286,396–398,531–540`). The “0 boxes below meanline” check compares against those same generated boundaries (`scripts/labelfix/s6_validate.py:174–178`; `tests/labelfix/test_s6.py:28–34`). It verifies a programmed bound, not that the bound follows the printed body. The original toolkit has per-column middle masks and curve evidence (Q1 table).

**Fix.** Implement S1/S2 with explicit physical proposal, recogniser association and uncertainty states. Use per-column band masks and keep source-pixel membership. Add independent drawn/native body-boundary witnesses, including curved lines, word-level size changes and wrong/duplicated CTC spans. Preserve S6's proven body-height repairs; do not count narrow or fallback partitions as physical certification. Assess mark recovery and body contamination separately.

### M07 — MAJOR: owner-page defaults do not reproduce the evaluated reader

**Evidence.** The live build path is `main → detect_s4 → R.read_ink` (`scripts/labelfix/owner_pages_build.py:270,195–202`). It passes recogniser text and alignment, not Google or truth. However, the default `--shape-model` is `docs/records/labelfix/markshape_model.json` (`238–245`), while S4–S6 validators use `markshape_model_S4.json` (`scripts/labelfix/s4_validate.py:47`; `scripts/labelfix/s6_validate.py:127`). The two checked files have different SHA-256 values (`24b155…` versus `9de4b4…`). The builder does not hash-bind its output to an iteration/model/filter policy.

Using the two assets on the same six native crops with the same current geometry changes **four of six raw readings**. For example `384c3f89-p000142-l0002` starts `ἥντινα` with S4 and `ήντινα` with the builder default. These are reproducibility differences, not a new accuracy comparison. The page also boxes raw physical evidence even when K1 removes the name (`scripts/labelfix/owner_pages_build.py:205–216`), and still numbers original connected components (`227,232`) while S6 may emit several character regions for one number.

**Fix.** Require an explicit versioned reading configuration or default to the accepted, hash-bound model. Generate page text and overlays from the same serialized `LineReading`, with raw detection, removed-by-constraint and final output clearly distinguished. Carry stable displayed slot IDs plus character ownership for fused/union cases. Add a build parity test against the chosen saved readings and a test that changing only Google/human fields cannot change “Ours.”

**Google check.** The unused `ours_line` and `reconstructed` helpers explicitly use Google and even keep Google marks (`scripts/labelfix/owner_pages_build.py:44–82`), but they are **not called by this main build path**. They should be removed or renamed and isolated as legacy correction displays. Their presence is not evidence that the current page's Ours line is directly Google-contaminated.

### M08 — MAJOR: owner/regression checks can certify the wrong thing or miss changes entirely

**Evidence and counterexamples.**

* `scripts/labelfix/owner_implicit_changes.py:39–52` skips missing lines, extracts every integer in free text as a slot, treats adjacent slots as mentioned too, and reconstructs slot ownership from the **current** analyser/CTC maximum overlap (`19–32`). It does not freeze old page identities or reject equal-overlap ambiguity. Thus an addition near a named slot can escape an implicit-regression label, and renumbering can change the judgment.
* `scripts/labelfix/box_regression.py:37–52` uses greedy order-dependent IoU matching. The probe's identical sets produce 1 lost + 1 added in one old-box order, versus 0 lost + 0 added + 2 moved when reversed. `29–34` always chooses the nearest letter, even for a distant side mark; added boxes use old letter ranges when present (`52`). `68` compares only the intersection of line IDs, so an entirely missing new line disappears from the check.
* `scripts/labelfix/s6_notes.py:69–89` checks known naming errors and coarse physical-zone preservation on empty-note lines. An unknown slot can lose its approved `acute` part while retaining its above blob and still pass; the probe demonstrates this. `no_top` also passes when the requested slot resolves to no characters (`105`). `safe_zone`, `fusion` and `mark` clauses (`90–109`) establish generated-bound compliance, region count/nonoverlap and blob counts, not full transcription correctness.
* R1 owner checks use older 59/33/56 sets (`scripts/labelfix/clariden/r1_owner.py:78–113`) rather than current 49/57 review/example notes. `no_regression` ignores unknown truths (`53–58`), and mark clauses can allow extra mark units (`46–50`). K1's unchanged 36/49 and 33/57 counts therefore do not independently establish that its output text preserved every accepted mark.

**Fix.** Bind notes to an explicit reviewed page/version, old slot geometry and structured clause targets. Do not infer review completion merely from an absent DB row; preserve any owner-authorized “empty means approved” convention only for a known reviewed snapshot. Use maximum-cardinality/weight one-to-one matching, report unmatched lines and ambiguous owners, and keep old/new slot namespaces separate. For approved-but-unscored marks, compare names/multiplicity as well as physical presence. Missing targets must be unresolved, not passed. Report physical, naming, segmentation and complete-note success separately, with the note-set version in every table.

### M09 — MAJOR: R1's comparison labels and scoring receipts do not bind the actual baseline/policy

**Evidence.** `docs/records/labelfix/spellcheck/clariden/R1_result_3678928/metrics.json:1544–1554` labels TP/FP/FN **3,731/245/322** as S5. Saved S5 actually has **3,714/249/339**; **3,731/245/322 is the saved S6 total**. The adapter checks line IDs and truth equality but not the supplied baseline's declared version, boxes, slot identities or metrics (`scripts/labelfix/clariden/r1_score.py:161–166`). Its source hashes include S4 only (`170`; stored `docs/records/labelfix/spellcheck/clariden/R1_result_3678928/metrics.json:10897–10900`). `prediction_map` verifies prediction content/counts but does not verify its receipt's extraction/index/checkpoint binding against the supplied metadata (`25–38`; compare the hashes written by `scripts/labelfix/clariden/r1_train.py:115–118`). `score_receipt` omits the S5 input, scoring source and constraint-policy hash (`228–230`).

Current scoring also applies K1 to R1 by default (`scripts/labelfix/clariden/r1_score.py:46,73–78`), whereas the reference columns remain frozen. It clears old naming confidence, correctly, but retains S4 physical evidence inside copied recogniser slots (`48,54–58`); `_mark_support` can rank R1 proposals using that old detector's blobs (`greekocr/labelfix/reading.py:89–114`). Produce raw and filtered arms separately, with symmetric reference comparisons. Do not rank R1 mark support using unrelated S4 blobs; export reader evidence/confidence or abstain when it is unavailable. Preserve the original raw result as a separate artifact.

### M10 — MAJOR: R1 admission is not an ink-completeness guarantee; window scale still depends on crop padding

**Evidence.** R1 estimates a median height from ink quantiles inside CTC spans, including all rows of the crop (`scripts/labelfix/clariden/r1_pixels.py:27–49`), then checks only relative span width and line confidence (`52–58`; `scripts/labelfix/clariden/r1_extract.py:54–64`). A blank crop falls back to CTC width/0.6 and can pass: the probe admits one alpha sample with `pixel_measurements=0`. The fallback is recorded, but does not produce an unresolved reading state. This does not justify restoring the forbidden connected-component gate; it shows that “admitted” must not be described as “visually verified.”

Horizontal window margins are correctly font-scaled, but the **entire original crop height** is anisotropically resized to 64 rows (`scripts/labelfix/clariden/r1_extract.py:19–32`). The same 20-pixel body becomes 32 dark rows in a 40-row crop and 16 in an 80-row crop when only blank padding changes. The existing test checks that the estimated font height is equal, but never checks the resulting two windows (`tests/labelfix/test_r1.py:26–37`). Top/bottom neighbouring-line ink can also influence the height and input. White padding is not distinguished from observed paper by the span mask.

**Fix.** Keep fused samples eligible; make no-pixel/partial/out-of-image evidence an explicit uncertainty state. Compare a baseline-aligned, font-normalized view with source-validity masks against the current view before any retraining or deployment. Add padding, skew, neighbour-line, clipped-mark and fused-boundary robustness tests. Report all geometry/fallback strata, and add an independently calibrated abstention/correction gate; argmax alone is not evidence that a Google correction is ink-justified. This is a robustness/admission defect, not proof that the current R1 score is wrong by a particular amount.

## Minor findings

### N01 — MINOR: historical validators now inherit changed runtime defaults

`scripts/labelfix/s4_validate.py:53`, `scripts/labelfix/s5_validate.py:53` and `scripts/labelfix/s6_validate.py:132` call current `read_ink` without `filtered=False`. Do not overwrite historical artifacts by rerunning old commands against mutable code.

### N02 — MINOR: sampling-arm names obscure two simultaneous changes and a selected source distribution

Arm D changes both the sampler and loss weights (`scripts/labelfix/clariden/r1_train.py:54–68,87–97`), so A versus D is not a pure sampling ablation. E's “natural” none share is natural to the selected C1 agreement pool, already enriched for Google-labelled compounds (`scripts/labelfix/clariden/c1_select.py:59–71`), not natural to deployment books. Five single-seed arms and tiny rare-class supports do not establish a reliable best-arm difference. **Fix:** label both sampling and weighting in comparisons; report source-pool versus deployment prevalence; use a controlled ablation and paired book-level uncertainty before choosing a winner. No additional compute was run for this review.

### N03 — MINOR: fallback and naming confidence are too easy to interpret as calibrated evidence

If the S5 case model is absent, `greekocr/labelfix/case.py:39–49` returns a heuristic class with probability 1.0 and no classifier-source flag in its returned record (`83–87`). S4 training calibrates and saves `model.tau` (`scripts/labelfix/s4_train.py:111–117`), but whole-line naming defaults to 0.5 and includes uncalibrated slope/shape fallbacks (`greekocr/labelfix/markshape.py:51,75,88–100`). For heuristic fallback names, the recorded probability is still the rejected model prediction's probability, not calibrated confidence in the chosen heuristic name. **Fix:** fail closed on required missing assets or expose a clearly unqualified fallback; attach method-specific confidence/provenance and avoid using unrelated probability as evidence rank. Calibrate a correction decision separately from diagnostic naming.
