# ASTRA-R2design — review 1

17 October 2026. Design recommendations only. No training, inference, adjudication calls, release rebuild, or repository changes were run. Counts, proportions and acceptance margins proposed below are experimental settings, not measured optima or claims that the required checked slots already exist.

## 1. Data mixes: make R2a a small native repair, then qualify synthetic coverage separately

**Recommend R2a = the frozen R1 training pool, with print-proven target repairs and at most 4,000 additional native supervised slots.** Do not add whole parliamentary, vouli or openarchives corpora to this paired run. Mine those sources for the specified failure mechanisms, alongside books outside every evaluation set. Oversampling agreement words alone is liable to reproduce the existing prior: the owner evidence records 15,230 breathing-bearing ο/οι versus 112 bare, while asking specifically about capital Α and small ο hallucinations. [OWNER_REMARKS_20261017.md:27–34,42–56][own27]

Use the following **2,000-negative / 2,000-positive repair budget**. A negative here is absence of the *target head*, not necessarily an entirely unmarked vowel. Every admitted slot needs checked labels for all four heads; unknown heads require an explicit masked loss, never a fabricated zero. Count distinct `(crop hash, recogniser NFD start)` slots once.

| Native repair slice | Target negative slots | Matched positive slots | Required distinction |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Capitals, all-caps words, initials and numeral-adjacent capitals | 800 | 500 | Bare Α/Η/Ο etc. versus genuinely printed capital breathings/accents; punctuation and keraia are not combining marks |
| Small ο/οι and other unmarked/monotonic print | 400 | 400 | Bare forms versus printed breathings; include εξ- without applying an orthographic rule |
| Subscript on α/η/ω, especially -η endings | 400 | 500 | Printed subscript absent versus present, including acute/circumflex compounds |
| Specks, show-through, neighbour ink and observed crop-edge distractors | 400 | 200 | Foreign ink versus a real mark owned by the queried slot |
| Exact-name contrasts | 0 | 400 | Grave/acute/circumflex; ΐ and other dialytika compounds; psili/dasia and crasis |
| **Total** | **2,000** | **2,000** | Source and font diversity within each slice |

These are acquisition targets. Start with 800 blind word cards, annotate their complete visible forms, and inventory the resulting usable slots. Continue only deficient slices to the target; stop at 2,000 word cards for this repair stage and report any shortfall. Do not multiply a few adjudicated anchors into thousands of “new” slots. Within a slice, cap a document at 10% and a page at five target cards where the inventory permits; lack of diversity is a reported limitation, not a reason to pull evaluation books into training.

**Use existing truth selectively.** The C1 new-source mark-error cards provide training candidates, after joining exact crop/slot identities, checking current stratum admission and converting the card's diagnosis into complete printed targets. Their original check allocated 50 cards to each of two readers within a stratum; that is not the same as two independent readings of every card. Re-adjudicate incomplete or single-read targets by the trusted two-reader/tiebreak method before supervision. C1 reports 101 mark-error cards and 172 neighbour-ink cards; it does not certify every slot on those lines. Failed-crop strata remain excluded even when one word is legible. [collect_v3/c1/C1_REPORT.md:3–30,41–44][c1]

**Keep all evaluation truth out of gradient updates:** the 666 and its 189-word print-truth overrides; the owner's 39 held-out cards, including the correction; S8 DEV's 600-line census and all associated cards; audit100; locked (ii) 200, (i-a) 133 and (i-b) 400 lines; their document/edition aliases and any explicitly reserved test material. Keep the original 16 C1 validation documents out too. DEV may identify a failure *category* and assess candidate arms; acquire different native examples for training. Audit100 is a selected correction audit, not a general-purpose training set. S8 contains 978 adjudicated word cards rather than complete truth for all 600 lines, and uses inclusion weights. [R1_REPORT.md:32–46][r1split]; [S8a6_DEV_TRUTH.md:3–35,59–65][dev]; [L1A2_AUDIT100_RESULT.md:3–19][audit]

**Use both mined and factory negatives, in different arms.** Native negatives lead because actual noise, print conventions and cropping caused the observed failures. Add a separate **SYN** arm with 40,000 distinct factory target slots: 8,000 capital-breathing positives, 8,000 subscript positives, 8,000 word-final grave positives, 8,000 dialytika positives (at least half dialytika+acute), and 8,000 matched target-head negatives. Pair marked/unmarked forms across the same font, size and nuisance settings; include real marked capitals in all-caps contexts when rendered, rather than teaching an all-caps prohibition. Vary neighbouring marks and word context without importing held-out sentences. Retain codec-v2 atoms, font hashes, shaping/coverage receipts and source-to-window coordinates. The factory checks codepoint coverage and rejects `.notdef`; additionally inspect final rasters of every new font × target combination, including whether the mark survives degradation. [factory/src/render.rs:75–84,198–202][factory]; [R1_mark_reader_outline.md:33–46][counts]

SYN adds about 2.0% of R2a's resulting unique pool, or 1.6% of the proposed R2b pool; its 8,000 negatives add about 0.4%/0.3%, respectively. R2a's native negatives are about 0.10% of its unique pool. These are **pool fractions**, not batch exposure under the recorded R1 sampler; record realised per-source/per-head draw counts as well. Do not impose a large hard-negative batch quota in the primary run. The separate HN arm in §4 tests that question.

**Cut crops are not automatic negatives.** If the relevant area is missing or unreadable, its label is UNKNOWN. Use partially clipped but still decidable examples only with print adjudication; place undecidable cuts in the abstention/robustness witness set. Do not train a hidden original mark as a visible positive after cutting it away, or train lost evidence as confirmed absence. R1's full-height resize and missing validity mask are documented defects; the owner specifically flagged clipped accents as “hallucination in some sense.” [ASTRA-SC1_review_1.md:145–151][m10]; [owner_sheet_notes_20261017.jsonl:7][notes]

**Confirmation/refutation:** R2a must lower false accepted capital/ο and subscript additions on disjoint native witnesses while preserving genuinely marked counterparts (§5). SYN must improve rare-class native recall/exact names across unseen books/fonts without increasing those false additions. A gain confined to rendered fonts rejects SYN as a real-print improvement.

## 2. Bootstrap labels: audited reader-led overrides, with agreement as a scalable but fallible base

**Recommend a frozen R1-A teacher, reader×Google agreement for the audited easy pool, and blind print truth for reader-led disagreement overrides.** Confidence prioritises and filters candidates; it does not make an unchecked disagreement true. Do not require recogniser mark agreement, detector ink, or K1 legality as another label vote. The recogniser supplies base-letter locations only. The owner's approval of reader-alone additions/substitutions supersedes the earlier audit's suggested detector insertion veto. [OWNER_REMARKS_20261017.md:7–24][ownrole]; [L1A2_AUDIT100_RESULT.md:20–28][audit]

The recipe must compare exact **head values**, including names, rather than mark presence or whole-word equality. Base-only, case-preserving Google/recogniser alignment may establish which Google target corresponds to a locator; mismatch/ambiguous alignment is excluded or separately print-resolved. It must not veto a reader correction because the recogniser's mark differs. R1's existing admission requires full NFD core equality, including marks; reusing that gate would silently defeat the proposed bootstrap. [r1_common.py:89–97][common]; [r1_extract.py:50–78][extract]

| Slot/head evidence | Recommended label action |
|---|---|
| Blind printed target available, full identity/ownership resolved | Use printed target, even when reader and Google both disagree; retain provenance |
| Reader = Google = named value; crop/locator checks pass; admission cell passed blind audit | Admit as **pseudo-label**, never “print truth”; keep full composed tuple for joint-noise audit |
| Reader = Google = none | Admit only from a separately audited shared-none head cell; cap **all-four-heads-none slots** at 60% of each new source's unique pool before sampling; retain random none controls and report rejection counts |
| Reader adds a mark Google lacks, or names another mark | Queue by calibrated confidence/margin; blind-adjudicate every admitted override; print target wins, including when Google wins |
| Reader says none but Google has a mark | No automatic deletion label; only print-adjudicated absence may override Google for training; release deletion policy remains separate and frozen |
| Low confidence, undecidable clipping, no observed pixels, uncertain slot ownership, unsupported atom | Exclude or mask the unresolved head; never coerce to none |

For the first R2b implementation, use **complete four-head targets only**: quarantine slots with any unresolved head. Partial-head loss masks are a later implementation option, not an unrecorded change to R2a. Likewise, do not satisfy the none cap by repeating the same rare positives. For the pseudo-label pool use provisional `p(top) ≥ 0.99` and top-minus-second probability ≥0.20 **per head**, then replace these starting cutoffs with values fixed on a separate print-labelled calibration cohort. Apply none confidence to the none class itself. Retain all logits/probabilities, margins, crop validity, teacher hash and calibration version. Current export saves only the selected nonzero value's probability, and labels it uncalibrated; the historical score artifact explicitly lacks confidence. Neither supplies reliable none probabilities or margins. A new teacher inference/export is necessary preparation, not evidence already available. [r1_train.py:100–120][trainpred]; [R1_result_3678928/RESULT.md:173–175][resulttail]

**Plain reader∧Google cannot repair shared omissions.** A printed subscript missed by both becomes an agreed none; a reader-correct/Google-wrong grave is discarded. Three-way mark agreement adds a correlated Google-trained voter and more rejection, not independent truth. Concrete shared-miss witnesses already exist in the held-out export, and the original SC1 finding explains the mechanism. [reader_errors_39.json:75–95,122–138][shared]; [ASTRA-SC1_review_1.md:48–56][m01]

The recommended route escapes that restriction through independently corrected disagreement slots, audits of the shared-none pool, and, if SYN qualifies, rendered positives. If a shared-none cell fails, quarantine that source/head cell, obtain independent labels or use qualified synthetic coverage; do not simply raise teacher confidence or add a third correlated vote. An entire missing class that both systems confidently omit requires random none sampling or independent factory/native positives to discover it.

**Audit before any student training.** Draw 1,600 blind word cards, each with one prespecified anchor slot and all four printed heads, from the proposed bootstrap population:

| Primary audit stratum, assigned in this order to avoid double counting | Cards |
|---|---:|
| Reader/Google disagree: 100 additions and 100 substitutions, balanced across weak heads | 200 |
| High-risk agreed labels: 100 capitals/all-caps/numeral contexts; 100 neighbour ink/show-through/crop-edge cases | 200 |
| Agreed positives: 100 each acute, grave, circumflex, psili, dasia, subscript, dialytika-only, dialytika+acute | 800 |
| Agreed-none head anchors: 200 uniform over eligible none-head opportunities (including slots whose other heads are positive); 200 from capital, ο/οι, -η and monotonic risk contexts | 400 |
| **Total, excluding controls** | **1,600** |

The primary sampling unit is a prespecified head at an anchor slot, with the complete tuple read for diagnosis. Construct exclusive primary cells before drawing, and retain the selection probability for each anchor; do not retrospectively count conveniently labelled neighbouring slots as random audits. Cross these with W1 source/stratum, old-wave versus new source, print era, case, and confidence bin (0.99–0.995 / 0.995–1). Allocate at least 100 cards to each admitted source family if the primary allocation permits; top up rather than silently pool a deficient source. Report weighted label-error rate and unweighted class/source tables. Inspect literal names, omission versus insertion, ownership and observability, plus composed-tuple error. Preserve UNKNOWN counts without calling them correct. Do not cap the none rate of every individual head at 60%: that would manufacture enormous subscript/dialytika prevalence.

**Admission gates are proposed engineering limits:** weighted label-error point estimate ≤0.5% and nominal one-sided 95% upper bound ≤1% overall; upper bound ≤2% for each named-value cell; shared-none omission upper bound ≤1%; source-family upper bound ≤3%. For the population estimate, combine stratum-level bounds with known population weights and simultaneous error allocation; do not apply a binomial interval to a weighted pseudo-count. Show individual exact binomial bounds as sampling diagnostics and document-cluster sensitivity alongside them; the nominal bounds do not account for correlated grader or book errors. Zero errors in 100 cards still permits about 3% error, so a 100-card cell does not meet the 2% gate. Top up zero-error named cells to at least 150 and none cells to at least 300; those are individual-cell minima, not a guarantee that all simultaneous gates pass. Use an independent fresh verification draw after any rule/threshold change. Allow up to 3,200 audit cards before the first student run. A cell still failing or too sparse at that budget stays out. Report actual supports and uncertainty rather than treating “no observed error” as qualification.

**Prevent a confirmation loop:** freeze the teacher and one immutable candidate manifest; keep every blind-audit card out of student training, even when it fails; admit overrides only with independent printed targets from a separate acquisition pool. Train students from scratch, with the old pool retained. Do not relabel the pool with the student, select confidence on locked tests, or regard agreement of differently seeded copies as independent truth. R2a as teacher is a later TEACHER arm after R2a passes: change only teacher identity and re-audit affected cells. New W1 documents are the strongest teacher-unseen source; remaining old-wave slots can be new slots from teacher-seen documents, so do not describe all bootstrap data as out of teacher sample.

**Stop rule:** one teacher→student generation in this round. Do not train a failing admission cell. Stop expansion after 500,000 accepted new unique slots, or the first audit-budget/cell-quality limit; never backfill with failed or unchecked strata. Reject a student that fails §5, even if pseudo-validation loss improves. Another generation requires fresh blind label-noise evidence and fresh development documents; improvement on the same exposed cards cannot justify it.

**Experiment that settles the policy:** compare R2b-EQ (audited agreement only) with R2b-OV (same accepted agreement pool plus print-verified disagreement overrides), on independently printed Google-omission and name-confusion strata. Report yield, label noise, native omission recovery and new false edits. This tests whether independent corrections add information; automatic confidence-only disagreement labels and triple-agreement are not the recommended production policy.

## 3. Old + new: retain all 300 training documents; preserve the 16-document validation boundary

**Mix, do not replace.** R1's 2,057,665 extracted samples include validation: the actual training log has **1,935,016 draws per epoch**. Keep all eligible old training slots, with only native-proven target replacement or unresolved-target removal. Corrections replace an old target once; they are not appended as contradictory duplicates. Keep the 316-document inventory, with 300 train and the same 16 validation documents; do not move the latter into training to reach a quota. [R1_mark_reader_outline.md:29–32,58–59][counts]; [A_train_log.csv:1–3][alog]; [r1_common.py:46–64][splitcode]

R2a targets approximately **1,939,016 distinct training slots** before replacements/removals: 99.79% retained old slots and 0.21% independently checked native additions. R2b-OV targets approximately **2,439,016**: 79.34% old, 0.16% repair additions, 20.50% newly admitted slots. Within the new 500,000, target 40% W1 admitted Greek strata (200,000), 40% remaining training-wave polytonic slots outside R1 (200,000), and 20% collection-v2 train monotonic/mixed-Greek slots (100,000). The last slice counters the tendency to add historical breathings to unmarked print. W1's 25,920 usable rows are a row inventory, not a guaranteed number of eligible Greek vowel/ρ labels. Reduce the final pool if these independent unique-slot budgets cannot be filled; do not duplicate rows or draw from dev/test to make the proportions exact.

Apply the **current per-stratum and line-level gate** before these quotas. Initially use C1-passed Greek W1 strata only; exclude pending mixed Greek/Latin and failed Gazette, kosmopolis and lekythos crop strata unless a later, hash-bound qualification actually admits them. Do not count Cyrillic-only/numeric rows as mark-reader expansion. The 271,987-line v2 total includes several scripts and dev/test splits; only the eligible train subset is available. The 124,669 training-wave lines already contain C1 rows, so union by identity and crop hash, not by adding the two advertised totals. [collect_v3/c1/C1_REPORT.md:5–33][c1]; [release_exclusions/unchecked_strata.json:1][stratum]; [collection/v2/CLEAN_REPORT.md:55–74][v2]; [census/changes/CHANGES.md:22–32][census]

Start with **unit source weight for old and independently supervised targets; 0.5 loss multiplier for newly admitted pseudo-labels**, independent of the existing per-head class weights. In R2b-EQ/OV, apply the same convention, with print-verified overrides at weight 1. Do not repeatedly oversample the small truth pool to 5–10% of every batch. Record unique counts, total draws and effective loss mass by provenance; pool percentages above are not effective training weights. A WEIGHT arm tests pseudo weight 1 instead of 0.5 if the audited pool qualifies but learning underuses it.

Keep new validation/calibration documents disjoint from every training document and from each other. Reserve a deterministic 10% of eligible new documents, stratified by source/era, **before** new-slot acquisition; the 500,000 target applies after this reservation. Preserve old natural-validation checkpoint selection for the strict R2a comparison. For primary R2b, use the same old-validation checkpoint criterion to avoid another simultaneous change, and inspect the new print-labelled development cohort only at the prespecified checkpoints/arm decisions. A new checkpoint-selection rule would be its own arm.

**Confirmation/refutation:** old-source per-class recall and error rates must remain within §5 margins while new-source printed-mark recovery improves. A REPLACE arm removing the old pool is warranted only if source-specific evidence implicates old label noise after targeted repairs; test it against the same update budget. There is no evidence here that replacing 300 documents is preferable.

## 4. Recipe and model: preserve the checkpoint recipe; isolate additions to it

**Resolve the recipe identity before launching anything.** The task context calls A natural-sampled, but both the job-bound configuration and job receipt say A was balanced, none=0.40, rare minimum=0.05; D was natural. The sampler confirms this and uses capped inverse-frequency head weights for A/E. This is a material contradiction, not an invitation to silently reinterpret A. Bind R2a to the **approved checkpoint's actual job recipe**; the available job-3678928 evidence points to balanced A. If a separate natural-trained approved A checkpoint exists, use its receipt and reroot these controls around it. [run_binding.json:3–40][binding]; [run_receipt.json:5–32][receipt]; [r1_train.py:40–97][sampler]

Keep 15 epochs, AdamW, lr=1e-3, weight decay=0.01, batch 512, bf16, 64-d case-preserving letter embedding, original window geometry and best checkpoint by unweighted natural validation loss. Use the same sampler and class-weight *calculation*, logging resulting weights rather than silently changing them. Train from scratch, not a fine-tune. R2a changes only the supervised data manifest. The code hard-codes the seed, so a later runner must expose model and sampler seeds while leaving the document split frozen; naming a new seed without changing both is not a seed arm. [r1_train.py:123–162,174–189][trainrecipe]; [r1_model.py:22–41][model]

**The single change I expect to buy most is native, print-verified counterexamples with marked counterparts**, especially subscript absence/presence and capital/ο contexts. That expectation is based on the observed prior skew and release errors, not a demonstrated training-data cause. R2a refutes it if target false edits do not fall across seeds, or marked-capital/subscript recall pays for the gain. Do not start by increasing model size.

R2b-OV therefore has up to **2,441,016** slots after its separately checked override tranche, less overlaps and removed targets. Do not relabel already admitted EQ targets to create the OV contrast: new independently labelled disagreement slots are its sole data delta. EQ versus R2a changes both pool size and new-pseudo weighting as a defined bootstrap treatment; it cannot isolate the 0.5 weight's effect. The WEIGHT arm changes only that multiplier. If finer attribution matters, add an old-pool-only parent with the same provenance-weight machinery (which is identity on old slots), and retain BUDGET.

**Why E lost:** the data support a measured tradeoff, not a causal story. In raw scoring E has breathing precision 97.96% versus A 98.74%, accent F1 99.59% versus 99.79%, but subscript recall 95.74% versus 94.68%; its total F1 is lower. E uses the natural none share **of a compound-enriched agreement selection**, retaining positive head balancing and loss weights. D changes sampling and loss weights simultaneously. Five single-seed results cannot show that all class balancing is bad or that more negatives are the cure. [R1_result_3678928/RESULT.md:9–16,173–175][result]; [ASTRA-SC1_review_1.md:159–161][samplingreview]

**Capital awareness:** case is already present in the embedding, so appending the same uppercase bit is not a compelling first change. CTX adds word context the embedding lacks. Crop-height/font-height ratio is a geometry diagnostic; the WIN arm addresses the demonstrated resize sensitivity more directly. Do not combine CTX, a wider crop, a validity channel and SYN in one purported model arm. Retain the 1.5-left/0.75-right margins initially: narrowing them already lost overall in C. If WIN adds a validity channel later, that is a separate WIN-MASK ablation. [R1_mark_reader_outline.md:9–14][window]; [r1_model.py:25–39][model]; [R1_result_3678928/RESULT.md:11–16][result]; [ASTRA-SC1_review_1.md:145–151][m10]

**Threshold priorities:** calibrate subscript additions and breathing/accent additions in capital/ο contexts first. Check acute↔grave/circumflex and psili↔dasia substitution reliability separately. ΐ needs joint accent+dialytika training/examples and exact composed-name scoring; raising an accent threshold cannot teach a missing dialytika feature. Crasis needs correct positional/printed-name targets. Missing crop information needs explicit uncertainty, not a more confident name. Elision, superscripts and em dashes are outside these four heads; keep them as surrounding distractors/preservation checks rather than claim R2 fixes them. Do not reuse the older shape-classifier script's held-out fitting/calibration pattern for R2. [OWNER_REMARKS_20261017.md:42–51][owncats]; [R1_REPORT.md:90–96][mapping]; [markshape_train.py:81–95][oldcal]

**0a:** skip an identical-seed training rerun by default. Replay the saved checkpoint through the bound extraction/scorer and compare frozen outputs/denominators first. Run 0a only if hashes/runtime drift or numerical nondeterminism needs diagnosis; it cannot estimate cross-seed variation. Do not train B again merely to recreate A∧B agreement; it changes the authorised reader-alone decision rule. Complete BASE/R2a, then the two bootstrap arms; promote only shortlisted treatments to three seeds and frozen tests.

**Compute:** the lead's 15-minute estimate is plausible for old-pool training compute, not for every variant end to end. The receipt records 829.6 seconds of A training and 2,081.5 seconds for the five-arm job schedule; the latter is not one arm's pure training time. Bigger pools, new teacher inference, extraction, audits and three-seed comparisons invalidate a blanket 1–1.5 GPU-hour total. Forecast by observed throughput × actual draws plus measured overhead; do not run training to settle this design review. [run_receipt.json:3–32][receipt]

## 5. Evaluation: paired named-mark outcomes, with locked decisions separate from continuity

**Recommend three-seed development comparisons, followed by one frozen final readout of shortlisted configurations.** Before training, hash-bind document splits, crop versions, recogniser prediction/alignment streams, targets and provenance, teacher, adapter, model, scorer and correction policy. Record the primary comparisons and margins below before any new evaluation output. Use the same fixed recogniser spans for baseline and candidate; a locator repair must have its own arm.

Locked-cohort membership is not a licence to invent missing mark truth. Score the actual independently adjudicated mark/word regions; retain uncovered regions as UNKNOWN. R0's letter-only cards establish base-letter outcomes, not full-line or mark targets. If the frozen locked assets lack a required mark stratum, acquire blind marks before exposing predictions, or report the claim underpowered. Do not decode “pixel-adjudicated lines” as complete four-head truth without checking annotation scope. [R0_REPORT.md:3–5,144–163][r0]

The witness deck's 400 contrasts should cover 100 capital/all-caps/numeral contexts, 100 ο/οι/monotonic contexts, 100 subscript contexts and 100 rare exact names/crasis, with marked and target-head-absent counterparts in each applicable slice. Add padding-only, mild skew and span-jitter views of the **same** source witnesses as robustness diagnostics, paired by source; count them as repeated views, not additional independent test examples. WIN must preserve source-coordinate ownership, record missing source extent, and improve both these diagnostics and native held-out observations. [ASTRA-SC1_review_1.md:145–151][m10]; [ASTRA-SC1c2_review_1.md:107–121][witness]

### Score two distinct outputs

1. **Raw reader:** four-head argmax, with exact named-value confusion matrices, atomic mark TP/FP/FN and presence P/R; misnaming creates an FP for the emitted name and FN for the true name. Also report complete-tuple accuracy on all and on marked slots, compounds, per-letter and capital/lowercase slices. In particular separate acute/grave/circumflex, psili/dasia, subscript, dialytika-only and dialytika+acute. A presence win that leaves names wrong is not a naming win.
2. **Fixed correction pipeline:** additions and substitutions from a single reader under the same frozen policy for both checkpoints; Google base text and the existing deletion policy are fixed. CAL is a separate policy column. Report correct additions, incorrect additions, correct substitutions, incorrect substitutions, recovered Google errors, regressions where Google was right, accepted-edit precision, edit yield, abstention and retained Google errors. Show subscript addition precision independently. The recogniser's diacritics and detector names are diagnostic strata only; neither vetoes authorised additions/substitutions.

### Proposed pass criteria

These are prospective engineering margins; the evidence does not establish their statistical power in the frozen tests. “Inconclusive” is a legitimate result and does not authorise adoption.

- **R2a target benefit:** at least 30% fewer false target-head additions on the prespecified native capital/ο/subscript-negative witnesses, at least five net fewer wrong target slots, and a paired document-cluster 95% interval excluding no improvement. Preserve the corresponding positive strata. If R1 makes too few target errors to measure this, expand the independent witness sample before claiming the repair works; do not replace the denominator with selected old failure cards.
- **R2b added value:** versus R2a, at least 20% relative reduction in named-mark FN on independently printed Google-omission strata, with paired interval supporting improvement and no increase in false accepted additions/substitutions beyond the noninferiority margins. EQ→OV must improve independently judged overrides, not merely reproduce the teacher more often. Evaluate OV's extra ≤2,000 labels against an equal-count extra-agreement control if claiming that label *kind*, rather than additional supervised count, caused the gain.
- **Common-class noninferiority:** for each head/value with at least 200 printed positive and relevant predicted-positive opportunities, the one-sided 95% paired lower bound on candidate-minus-baseline precision must exceed −0.20 percentage points and on recall must exceed −0.50 points. Report exact positive and negative support; control the family of class checks, for example by Holm adjustment. Do not pool a clear failure in one source into a global pass.
- **Sparse classes:** below those supports, require no net increase in exact-name errors and review every paired regression, but label statistical noninferiority **unestablished**. Acquire fresh native cards where rare-class claims are necessary; synthetic test success or four historical dialytika examples cannot meet a native-data gate. A safety-critical sparse stratum can remain in review/abstention without rejecting unrelated qualified edits.
- **Non-target protection:** paired non-target exact errors and accepted-edit error must meet the same applicable margins; no confirmed regression on a preserved owner witness can disappear inside an aggregate. Show every such regression and disposition. Merely having fewer changed slots than BASE seed churn is not a pass: seed churn contains improvements, harmless name changes, and harms.
- **Coverage:** for CAL, compare error at matched edit coverage and coverage at a fixed risk bound; a higher precision obtained by refusing most additions is not reader improvement. Require ≥95% of baseline correct accepted edits to remain accepted overall and ≥90% within each supported target class. Separately report clipped/unknown abstentions. A raw-model gain and a policy-only gain get different claims.
- **Seeds:** use 20261016/17/18 with the split fixed, paired baseline/candidate runs at each seed. Report mean, min/max, each seed's gain/loss counts, and book-cluster paired intervals; do not count three predictions of one word as three independent truths. Require target benefit in at least two seeds, a positive mean, and no seed exceeding the non-target margins. The final fixed configurations are all three seeds, not the test-selected best seed.
- **Final readout:** apply the frozen criteria to (i-b) and (ii) separately and publish their uncertainty. Use (i-a), 666 and DEV for the labelled roles above. With only five fresh books, document-level intervals and source diversity may be limiting; report that limit rather than manufacture confidence from thousands of correlated slots. If the final readout drives another design change, the changed version needs another untouched cohort.

For intervals, use paired resampling of documents with original inclusion weights, with line-cluster sensitivity and sparse-cell bounds alongside it. Preserve all books, including undefined cells; do not average only books where the candidate emitted a mark. Final weighted estimates must reflect sampling fractions when known. The S8 method already supplies a usable model for recording these weights and limits, including reader error not covered by the intervals. [S8a6_DEV_TRUTH.md:31–45][dev]

**Attribution:** R2a−BASE estimates the native repair package, not each of its categories. R2b-EQ−R2a estimates audited expansion; OV−EQ tests independent disagreements; SYN−its parent tests factory additions; CAL compares policies on identical logits; WIN uses the identical data manifest. Only if R2a works and finer attribution is needed, run leave-one-repair-slice-out arms. Keep BUDGET before claiming a bootstrap gain is due to better labels. Do not combine all additions, adapter and thresholds into “arm 3” and attribute its gain to bootstrapping.

Before release handoff, rebuild only the frozen winner, compare every line and every changed output atom, preserve punctuation/base characters, and perform a **fresh 100-line correction audit** with wave/class sampling weights. Add unmatched lines and uncertain ownership to the box regression output. Since the reader does not produce detector boxes, unchanged boxes mainly verify integration; they cannot certify names or source ink. Audit100's prior 83/100 result and 13 subscript-insertion failures show why the final text audit is necessary. [L1A2_AUDIT100_RESULT.md:11–23][audit]; [ASTRA-SC1c2_review_1.md:107–115][witness]
